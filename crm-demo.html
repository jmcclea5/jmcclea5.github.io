Overview

How good a target the account is right now, from everything on the page: signals (PSA, Sumble alerts from your email, Demandbase) count most, then inbound leads and marketing activity, past replies and connects, meetings and an open opportunity. The total is then adjusted for how hard the account has been hit lately: sprinting now ×0.55, touched in 15 days ×0.7, 15–30 days ×0.85, 30–45 days ×1.0, 45–60 days ×1.08, 60+ days ×1.15, never ×1.1. Strong signals on an account nobody has touched in a while score highest.

Signals

PSA alerts from Salesforce (last 180 days) and Sumble alerts from your email, weighted by type and faded by age (full weight ≤30d, 70% ≤60d, 45% ≤90d, 20% ≤180d; repeats of the same type count half). Demandbase adds up to 25 points. Points are then eased onto 0–100, so one fresh funding round lands near 58 and two strong recent signals near 80.

    Meetings (12 mo)

    Past calendar events on the account in the last 12 months, excluding reschedules.
    • 1 → 35 · 2 → 50 · 3–4 → 65 · 5–7 → 78 · 8+ → 88
    • +12 if a meeting is on the calendar
    • +8 if the last meeting was in the past 30 days
    • None held but one scheduled → 30
